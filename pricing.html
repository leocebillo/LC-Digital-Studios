Questions

Pricing FAQ

Which pricing applies to me?

New business pricing is for businesses that are just starting out. Established business pricing is for businesses that have been open for a while. Not sure? Ask and we'll figure it out together.

What does monthly maintenance cover?

Updates, bug fixes, small changes to text and photos, and making sure your site or app keeps working. When something needs to change, you just let us know.

Why do I pay the domain and app store fees?

Paying these yourself means your web address and app listings are registered in your name, so you always own them.

How much is social media and photography?

It depends on how often you want posts and photo shoots, so we price these per project. Reach out and we'll put together a quote.
